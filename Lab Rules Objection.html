Lab Rules Objection
Public objection · G.S.R. 864(E)

Object to the draft lab animal rules

The draft would let labs approve most experiments on every species in-house, drop the 1998 rule making anaesthesia mandatory, and end funded rehabilitation of animals after experiments. Your letter below cites each rule and the objection for the same. Add your name, check it, and send it from your own email. (P.S. I have taken the template from @socktalks.tv)
